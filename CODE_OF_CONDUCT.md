## Code of Conduct

### Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion (or lack thereof), or sexual identity
and orientation.

This project explicitly welcomes participants across religious and
non-religious backgrounds, including practitioners and non-practitioners of
Buddhism and other traditions studied in this project's research. Studying
a tradition's ethics is not an endorsement of, or requirement to hold, that
tradition's beliefs, and no contributor should be pressured in either
direction.

### Standards

Expected:
- Respectful, evidence-based disagreement, including on contested ethical
  and philosophical questions.
- Constructive criticism of ideas, not people.
- Acknowledging uncertainty rather than asserting unsupported certainty
  (this applies to conduct discussions as much as to research claims).

Not acceptable:
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Dismissing a contribution because of a contributor's religion, nationality,
  or affiliation rather than its content.
- Using this project as a platform for religious proselytizing or for
  advocacy unrelated to its research scope.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.

### Enforcement

Report violations to the Core Maintainers via a private channel (a contact
address will be published in `docs/governance-members.md` once populated;
until then, open a GitHub issue marked `conduct` requesting private
follow-up, or contact the repository owner directly via GitHub).
Maintainers will review and respond within a reasonable timeframe and may
warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban contributors for violations,
following the process described in `GOVERNANCE.md`.
